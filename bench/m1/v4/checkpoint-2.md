## 2. Is the engine frame a trustworthy look gate?

**For geometry, rig, materials and tone: yes. For look: not yet, and it is still SwiftShader.** Nobody has captured a frame on the Air; `texture_mb_est` is 602 MB on the PNG path and the KTX2 copy is not what the gate renders.

Missing, in the order they distort judgement:

1. **Hair self-shadowing.** The rim lights every top card unoccluded; in Cycles ~80 % of hair luminance is rim TT through a shadowing mass. This is why the crown reads flat brown. Fix: cards cast into a hair-only shadow map per hair twin (depth from the light, PCF, card alpha ≥ 0.5), plus baked per-card light-occlusion along ±X/±Y/±Z from the groom density as a fallback. ~1 day.
2. **Fill is an unshadowed spot** (1.4× hair, 1.3× beard). Shadow it (one more map) or model it as a shadowed spot diffuse + LTC spec, the hybrid T6 named. 0.5 day.
3. **No AO**: the 0.5 direct-AO multiply is a hack and cards have none under the jaw/behind the ear. GTAO at half-res, 1 day. This also fixes the beard's hard cheek line partly (occlusion under cards).
4. **Hero-rig closeup is not captured** (`engine_shots.mjs` line 231 forces `rig = v === 'portrait' ? 'hero' : 'neutral'`). The only hero-light engine frame is the 1920×1080 portrait, where the head is ~300 px. 20 lines.
5. **Portrait framing mismatch.** Camera data is identical (85 mm, sensor 24 VERTICAL, shift −0.07/−0.16, same `aim=eyes` spec) and the viewer applies the shift (`stage.ts:445`), yet Cycles frames the head lower and larger. Untested hypothesis: Cycles renders at 50 % with its own aim re-solved on the hero meshes after `wet.gaze` / hair, vs the exported target solved at lookdev time. Test: overlay the eye landmark positions from both frames; 1 hour. Until then the portrait SSIM and the "hair beside the face" boxes compare different crops.
6. **Air capture** has never run. Everything in §1 could change on real anisotropic filtering, real MSAA resolve and real frame time. One 10-minute user session.
7. **Motion/TAA**: `idLodBias −2` will shimmer; no TAA; no device check. Not a still-frame issue, but the gate frames should carry a short turntable capture so shimmer is seen before more hair shading work.
8. Smaller: no DoF for the beauty portrait; AgX grey-chart check still not run; SSIM row stale.

After 1–5 the engine portrait is the gate for everything except the suit (which needs a full-body frame; the neutral `game_three_quarter` already does).

## 3. Open decisions

**C2: painterly-stylised realism on the character. Recommendation unchanged and stronger.** Today's evidence: every remaining gap (waxy skin, card hair without volume shadow, fur beard, strokes for brows, a lofted suit) is a *continuous-shading* gap, and a painted albedo + 3-band ramp + ink edge hides exactly those while keeping the form work that was done today. The Cycles `*_paint.png` variants already exist as the target. The realism path needs, before "believable": strand or strand-like hair with self-shadow, skin micro-normals, GTAO, DoF, the full suit rebuild, and probably hair-card geometry at 2× the budget: 2–3 weeks on top of the next round. The painterly path costs 2–3 days (albedo bake, ramp, ink pass in TSL) + 1 day to re-point the parity gates at the painted Cycles variant. Caveat the user should hear: stylisation does not fix *form*; head (done), suit (not done) and hands still need their work, and the beard grey read is a design value, not a shading one.

**Suit: go on spike 2, tighter than proposed.** The clay sheet is honest: today the spike looks worse than the lofted jacket (frilled collar, hinge seams, back cling), but sleeve pitch and skirt hang are real and *cannot* be reached by lofting. Spike 2 at 1.5 days with exactly the three fixes (two-stage weld, hull back collider, non-simulated collar/lapel) and a hard gate: front/¾/back clay beats lofted, zero seam crease, collar strain not measured because not simulated. **Do not pre-commit to the 11–13 day rebuild**; after spike 2 the user picks between (a) the rebuild, or (b) a 2-day "lofted v2" (modelled lapel roll, shoulder pad reduced, sleeve pitch by a rest-pose bend, back band removed) which under a painterly C2 might be enough. The decision needs the user because it is the largest single item left in m1 and depends on how much of the game is full-body third-person (my assumption: most of it, which favours the rebuild).

Listening gate, ArcFace, m1 base: no new evidence; ArcFace ruling should say "scalar-only, offline, in private, on the sheet" so T7's practice becomes policy.
