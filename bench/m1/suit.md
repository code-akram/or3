# m1 suit track: black three-piece, parametric rebuild

The suit is now **built on the body by script** (`blender/character/suit_build.py` primitives, `suit_jacket.py`, `suit_inner.py`, `suit_shoes.py`, `suit_textures.py`, driven by `suit.py`). Only the trousers still come from the CC0 suits01 shell (`suit_ops.tailor_trousers`). Every size is a parameter in `suit.DEFAULTS` and lands in `character.stats.json["suit"]`. Nothing is traced from film stills: the target below is written in words from costume-designer interviews and tailoring breakdowns, then built from body landmarks and the rig.

Runs (main checkout, `data/build/lookdev/`): before `20261012T104705Z-suit2-baseline` (the m1 wave-2 suit on the direction config), after `20261012T121526Z-suit2-final` (loop) and `20261012T121551Z-suit2-final-signoff` (Cycles, same build), compare sheet `compare-20261012T104705Z-suit2-baseline--20261012T121526Z-suit2-final/`.

**Fix round (2026-10-12, after the integration review of `20261012T121947Z-integ-all-1`)**: the collar region was shards, the knot a glossy ball, both shoulders had see-through holes and read as winged flaps. Fixed in `20261012T141459Z-suit3-final` (loop) and `20261012T141817Z-suit3-final-signoff` (Cycles); the loop run dir holds `suit_views/` (incl. the new `closeup_h`, `notch`, `collar_back`, `collar_side`, `armpit*` views), `pose_test/` and `suit_check/`. The suit-only iterations are in `data/build/suitdev/00-baseline` … `25-pitwrap`. What changed:

- **Collar and neck** (defect 1). The shoulder ring was a trapezius slice stretched in x (a thin wedge whose sides the collar fall, the lapel tips and the shirt leaves all crossed); it is now the deltoid ring (widest slice through the humeral heads, narrowed in x to the shoulder line, depth × 0.78), the neckline sits 2 cm higher (`back_neck_m` −0.022) and is lifted over the trapezius. The lapel's top edge tilts up over its last 30 % of rows (no vertical "tip wall"); the collar's end columns lie in the lapel's plane and the fall edge turns up at `notch_deg` 65° to the collar point, so the notch is an open wedge between two edges. The collar stand fades to 4 mm at the gorge point. The shirt band is ray-cast from the neck axis (the slice max-radius picked up the jaw and trapezius: the band was outside the jacket collar at the back), the leaves lie on the chest at `lay_m` 6 mm instead of standing out, and a shirt **yoke** (two draped panels, tucked under the jacket) fills what shows between the collar, lapels and sleeve heads. The stock shell's delete mask (cut for the stock collar) is dropped; `hide_body` keeps the neck. `collar_rested_verts`, `neckline_verts_lifted`, `shirt_yoke_tucked_verts` land in stats.
- **Tie knot** (defect 2). A four-in-hand: 38 × 44 × 17 mm (was 40 × 46 × 24: a ball), front a flattened arch (depth 0.45 of the width), left side 8 % fuller, top edge tilted 3 mm, hard-edged flat top under the collar, the back in the collar band; blade 25 → 78 mm. Silk roughness 0.35 → 0.48, sheen 0.15 → 0.10.
- **Shoulder holes** (defect 3). The sleeve head ring was the arm profile at the shoulder, ray-cast onto the block only where the ray hit: under the shoulder point the ring stood off the block and the quad strip between them was open (the background triangles in `front_mask.png`). The **scye** is now a loop found on the block's surface (horizontal rays from inside, from the shoulder point down the front and back to the armpit) and the head rows morph from it into the tube; `scye_misses_*` = 0. `bench/m1/suit_check.py` (below) is the regression test: 0 enclosed background components in 15 silhouettes, 0 open boundary edges in the armhole zone.
- **Winged shoulders** (defect 4). Rows between the deltoid top and the shoulder ring were horizontal slices of a sloping shoulder (they folded over each other and through the skin: 206 shoulder vertices were inside the body). The block now stops at the shoulder-point height (the body's top over the deltoid, by rays, + a 10 mm pad), the ring runs straight from the neck point down to it, and the sheet between the last row and the ring is interpolated by column and lifted over the trapezius/deltoid (`yoke_rows` 4). Between the pit and the shoulder point the slices enclose the deltoid out to the scye line, and the sleeve head never comes inside the body's exit radius + ease. Shoulder drop at the point 0.8 mm (the body's own slope; `slope_m` is gone), `shoulder_over_body_m` still 0.024.
- **Trousers** (5): not done, still the stock shell (see §5).

**Collar round (2026-10-12, after `20261012T141817Z-suit3-final-signoff`)**: the fix round's close-up still showed the collar zone broken (shirt collar as torn white shards, skin at the front of the neck, jagged jacket lapel/collar flaps, a dull blob of a knot). Rebuilt in `20261012T160641Z-collar-final` (loop; `pose_test/` inside) and `20261012T161011Z-collar-final-signoff` (Cycles). Suit-only iterations: `data/build/suitdev/c00-base` (= main 35fb1be) … `c30`. Root causes found with the new guard (`suit_collar.py`, §6) and per-panel debug colours (`SUIT_DEBUG_TAGS=1`):
- The neck had no shared frame: the shirt band, the jacket neckline and the jacket collar each measured the neck their own way (a slice centred well forward of the neck, so the "radius" was 2 cm at the front and 10 cm at the back). The jacket neckline sat ~5 cm off the back of the neck and the shirt pieces crossed it. Now `suit_neck.NeckFrame` fits one forward-leaning axis to the neck (front/back ray midpoints), samples the body radius r(a, z) by rays from it (96 angles × 50 heights), and every collar piece is placed in it with a fixed radial order: skin + gap < shirt band < shirt fall < jacket stand < jacket fall.
- The jacket neckline jumped ~5 cm from each gorge point to the neck circle in one column (a fan/spike at the gorge); now 6 interpolated gorge columns, then the seam is raised toward 10 mm under the band's base and kept 12 mm off the neck **and outside the band's base**.
- The lapel's top rows stacked into shelves (the yoke rows bunch within 5 mm under the gorge), and the tip rise and collar point made upward spikes: rows closer than 6 mm along the roll line are skipped, the rise is a smooth function of height (10 mm over the top 5 cm).
- The jacket collar was a vertical wall at the front; its front ends floated off the chest. It is now a draped construction (`suit_neck.build_jacket_collar`): per column a seam → fold stand (fold 14 mm under the shirt band's top, clear of the shirt and the body) and a fall marched down over the stand and the block by rays from the axis; the front 9 % at each end is a Coons patch from the gorge seam along the lapel's top edge to the notch and the collar point (28 mm at 30°) into the natural fall; the fall rests 3.5 mm on what is beneath, and block/lapel vertices that poke through the fall between its vertices are pressed 2 mm under it.
- The shirt collar was separate leaves standing up like a crown with a gap across the front showing skin, and a yoke that z-fought the front panel. Now (`suit_neck.build_shirt_collar`) one grid: band (base found where the neck meets the trapezius, on the body there + 5 mm, not on the slope below it), fold and fall with two 75 mm spread points (38°), a closure patch across the front under the knot, and two root strips that follow the skin from inside the band down 4 cm over the neck root (so what shows between the shirt collar and the jacket collar is shirt). The front panel runs 3 mm up inside the band. No yoke.
- The knot was a sphere-ish loft; now 10 rows × 17 segments, 36 × 46 × 15 mm, tapered 42 % to the bottom, the wearer's left 10 % fuller, a diagonal wrap step and a 3 mm skew, the back against the band. Silk roughness 0.62 (matte). The **tie blade's normals pointed into the shirt** (back faces toward every camera since wave 2): flipped.

## 2. How it is built

All garments are **lofted from cross-sections of the unmasked body** (`suit_build.Body`, captured in `suit.add` with the delete masks muted). A slice is a polar profile (max radius per angular bin), smoothed, **canvassed** (chord-filled so the cloth bridges the sternum and the small of the back instead of following the anatomy) and eased outward by the tailoring allowance. Rows of rings become quad grids (`suit_build.Grid`) with **metric UVs** (metres along the ring and up the loft), one rectangle per panel, so the texture pass can place seams by (row, column). Skin weights come from the nearest body vertex of the garment's bone set, so the layers deform together.

**Jacket** (`suit_jacket.py`):
- *Block*: ~47 rows from the hem to the shoulder ring, 72 columns from the left front edge round the back to the right front edge. Ease: chest 24 mm, back 14, sides 20, waist 16/13/14, hips 18, plus 10 mm of flare at the hem. Below the hips the slice is the running max of the slices above (the skirt hangs straight). Between the armpit (11.5 cm below the shoulder joint) and the shoulder point the slices take in the deltoid out to the scye line and are widened in x to the shoulder point (body bideltoid + 2 × 12 mm), with the side ease closing to that allowance. No horizontal row lies above the shoulder-point height (the body's top over the deltoid + a 10 mm pad): the shoulder ring is the deltoid slice narrowed to the shoulder line, running from the neck point down to the shoulder point, and the sheet between it and the last row is interpolated by column and draped over the trapezius and deltoid (`yoke_rows`). The neckline is lifted over the trapezius too.
- *Front*: the left panel overlaps the right by 2 cm below the fastening button, the right is tucked 3 mm inside; the quarters cut away 2.5 cm toward the hem. Above the button each front edge is the roll line, a straight V from the button to the neck point (±7.2 cm).
- *Lapels*: a flap folded back along the roll line, one row per block row: fold bulge (12 mm roll height, strongest mid-way), width growing from 0 at the button to 8 cm at the gorge (power 2.2, so the roll is soft), a 3 mm edge lip; over the last 30 % of the rows the top edge tilts up 26 mm so the lapel point sits above the gorge, the point standing 2 mm off the chest.
- *Neckline and collar* (`suit_neck.py`, in the shared neck frame): the top row runs from each gorge point through 6 interpolated columns onto the neck, raised toward 10 mm under the shirt band's base, 12 mm off the neck and 4 mm outside the band's base, lifted over the trapezius. The collar: a stand from that seam to a fold 14 mm under the shirt band's top (outside the shirt collar by 3 mm and the body by 12 mm), and a fall (42/40 mm side/back, at least the stand + 8 mm) marched down over the stand and the block by rays from the neck axis, resting 3.5 mm on them; the front 9 % at each end is a Coons patch between the gorge seam (along the lapel's top edge to the notch, 30 mm), the collar's leading edge (28 mm at 30° to the **collar point**), the fold and the first natural column, laid onto what is beneath; a 3 mm lip turned under. Block/lapel vertices that would poke through the fall are pressed under it.
- *Vents*: two 24 cm slits at ±0.55 of the back hem half-width, the outer flap lifted 4 mm (tapering to 0 at the top), and a 3.5 cm **underlap** strip behind each.
- *Sleeves*: 28-column tubes along the arm bones (upper arm, elbow blend, forearm) to 5 mm past the hand joint; radius = arm profile + 10 mm ease, never thinner than a 66 → 46 mm taper; a 3.5 mm rope on the top of the head. The **scye** is a loop on the block's surface (horizontal rays from inside the block, from the shoulder point down the front and the back, ±7 cm, to the armpit depth), so the head is sewn to the block all round; the 7 head rows morph from the scye's shape into the tube while sliding along the arm, and no sleeve vertex comes inside the body's exit radius + ease (the deltoid, the axillary folds). A 4 mm cuff lip.
- *Pockets*: two flapped hip pockets (16 × 6 cm, 7.5 cm below the button) and a slanted breast welt (10.5 × 2.4 cm), as thin panels standing 0.5–3.5 mm off the block.

**Inner layers** (`suit_inner.py`), built only where they can show (look-spec §4.3):
- *Shirt*: front panels inside the waistcoat's V plus a 3 cm margin (their top 3 mm up inside the collar band), a placket strip 1.2 mm proud with two buttons. The **collar** (`suit_neck.build_shirt_collar`) is one grid round the neck in the neck frame: a band from its base (where the neck meets the trapezius, at least 15 mm over the suprasternale at the front, rising to the back; radius = the body there + 5 mm) up 34/40 mm front/back, a fold, and a fall 3 mm over the band with two 75 mm **points** spread 38° whose tips blend into the side edge; a closure patch across the front under the knot; two root strips from inside the band down 4 cm over the neck root, 4.5 mm off the skin, from 17° to 115° either side of the front. Anything the jacket would cover is pulled inside it radially. No yoke (`yoke`: false). Cuff rings 13 mm past the sleeve ends.
- *Tie*: a **four-in-hand** knot (`suit_neck.build_knot`: 10 rows × 17 segments, 36 mm wide, 46 mm tall, 15 mm deep, 42 % narrower at the bottom, the wearer's left 10 % fuller, 3 mm skew, a 1.5 mm diagonal wrap step, the back against the band and the shirt front) and a blade from 21 to 78 mm wide with a 5 mm dimple under the knot, its top tucked into the knot, lying 3 mm over the shirt placket, cut 3 cm under the waistcoat V. Matte silk (roughness 0.62).
- *Waistcoat*: front panels from the vest V (top button 11.8 cm above the jacket button) out to the jacket's V + 5 cm, 8 mm off the body and pushed 3 mm clear of the shirt and tie; hem 2.5 cm below the waistband at the sides, points 5 cm lower with a notch; a 2.5 mm edge lip; 5 buttons, buttonholes and two welt pockets in the normal map; a 12 cm lower back panel for the vents.
- *Buttons* (`suit_buttons`): 8-segment discs, 22 tris each: 2 front, 4 per cuff (kissing, on the hindarm side), 5 vest.

**Shoes** (`suit_shoes.py`): black calf **cap-toe derbies**. Rings across each foot at 26 slices (polar half-profiles of the foot, chord-filled into a stiff last, smoothed along the foot, 4 mm leather), a toe box that extends 2.6 cm past the toes on an elliptical taper, the rings opened above a 64 mm topline behind the throat. A **welt** lip (5 mm) and a sole slab (10 mm, 26 mm heel block), three eyelets a side with criss-cross lace tubes. Cap seam, quarter seams, heel counter seam and welt stitch go to the leather normal map.

**Trousers** (`suit_ops.tailor_trousers`, stock shell): quarter break (back hem 3.5 cm), **leg-opening taper** to 9.5 cm half-width from the knee, front and back crease ridges, cylindrical metric UVs per leg with the outseam, inseam, hem edge, jetted back pockets and crease lines in the normal map.

**Surfaces** (`suit_textures.py`): the metric panel rectangles of each material group are shelf-packed into one square atlas (uniform texel density) and the UVs rewritten. A float **height map** is drawn with numpy: a 2/1 twill weave (1.9 mm period, a little over 2 px) plus two octaves of fibre grain, then the marks the builders registered in panel space (pressed seams with pick stitches either side, topstitched finished edges, rolled edge lips, bar-tacked buttonholes with a keyhole, crease ridges, welts), and converted to a **tangent-space normal map**. Groups and sizes: jacket 2048² (0.71 mm/px), waistcoat + trousers 2048² (0.77 mm/px), shirt 1024² (0.53 mm/px), tie 512², shoes 1024² (0.44 mm/px). Images are packed into the .blend and embedded by the glTF exporter. Materials: `suit_wool` (albedo 0.028, roughness 0.70, sheen tint 0.08), `suit_wool_b`, `suit_shirt` (0.75, 0.55), `suit_silk` (0.02, 0.62, sheen 0.12: matte), `suit_leather` (0.012, 0.28), `suit_sole`, `suit_button`. The glTF exporter writes Sheen *Tint* as `sheenColorFactor` and ignores Sheen Weight, so the strength goes in the tint.

Degenerate faces (zero vector area: seam wraps and collapsed lapel rows) are removed in `Garment.finish`; they exported zero tangents, which the Khronos validator flags as an error.

## 4. Poke-through test (look-spec §4.3 [auto])

`bench/m1/suit_pose_test.py` imports the GLB, drives the rig through nine poses (rest, the spike-1 stress pose, twist both ways, arms up, reach, crouch, lean, kick), renders a flat **ID pass** (one emission value per mesh, no AA) from four cameras and counts pixels per mesh. Inner layers (waistcoat, shirt, tie) may only show where they show at rest, so the count over the rest count is the poke-through estimate. Output: `poke.json`, `pose_<pose>_<view>.png` (IDs), `beauty_<pose>_<view>.png`.

## 5. Remaining weaknesses (honest)

- **Jacket skirt in leg raises** still lifts with the thigh (the skirt is weighted 50 % to the legs below the hips so it follows the walk; in a −50° thigh raise the front panel rides up and the inside shows). This is the `glute_helper` / Dem Bones job (look-spec §5.2), not fixable by rest-pose geometry.
- **Collar zone** (collar round): reads as a spread shirt collar, a knot and a rolled jacket collar in the harness close-up, but it is not tailoring-grade. The jacket collar's front ends are a Coons patch: seen edge-on from the close-up camera, the wearer's right end still shows as a thin grey blade beside the shirt collar, and the notch has a small ragged corner where the collar end meets the lapel's top (the block shows through for a few mm). The fall carries faint diagonal shading streaks on the wearer's left (fall rows resting on an uneven block), and in the Cycles close-up its outer edge near the notch is a stepped dark line (the turned-under lip follows the Coons rows); the wearer's right lapel top shows crumpled shading where it rolls toward the gorge. 66 jacket triangle intersections remain (collar fall vs stand at the sides under the hair, collar end vs block at the gorge) and 34 shirt ones (front panel / closure under the knot), all hidden at rest. The shirt band at the sides sits on the neck root, which flares fast there (the band is a cone, base 8–10 cm vs top 6–7 cm from the axis); the neck inside the band shows through the opening beside the knot (reported by the guard as neck_inside_px, not gated). The knot has no real dimple, only a crease line.
- **Lapel notch** is an open wedge between the lapel's top edge and the collar's leading edge; the gorge seam is not sewn (the collar end lies 1.5 mm over the lapel's top edge) and there is no edge stitch along the notch.
- **Armpit**: a ~2 mm sliver of skin shows at the back of each armpit from below and behind (`suit_views/armpit_back.png`): the sleeve's bottom passes through the armpit cavity where a real jacket has a gusset. Wrapping the under-pit arm vertices into the block did not change it (`25-pitwrap`); it needs a gusset panel between the sleeve bottom and the side body, or a lower armhole with a wider sleeve bottom.
- **Pose test counts** went up with the yoke and the kept neck (§4); the test's rest-subtraction cannot tell "more collar opening" from poke-through, so the beauty frames are still the arbiter.
- **Shirt collar points**: they lie on the chest under the jacket; where the hair falls over them they intersect the hair cards (hair is not an occluder).
- **Sleeve pitch**: the sleeves follow the bone axis, so in the A-pose they hang straight; a real sleeve is pitched forward about 10°. Add a pitch parameter once the rest pose is re-evaluated against the locomotion set.
- **Trousers** are still the stock shell: the rise, seat and fly are the stock mesh, and the UVs are cylindrical per leg (seams in the normal map only).
- **Normal-map weave** is at the Nyquist edge of a 2k atlas (2.4 px per period); it mips to flat at distance, which is fine, but at the sign-off close-up it shows as fine diagonal hatching rather than cloth.
- **Shoes**: the upper's topline is a straight cut; no tongue, no heel pull; the throat/facing geometry is implied by the eyelets and laces. The sole bottom is a flat cap.
- Materials still have no base-colour textures (flat albedo + normal). Shoe leather would benefit from a roughness map (toe-cap polish).

## 6. Tools

- `bench/m1/suit_dev.py`: the suit loop (build without hair in one Blender process, render the detail views in another): `blender -b -t 4 --factory-startup --python bench/m1/suit_dev.py -- --out DIR [--override '{"suit": {...}}'] [--views …] [--wire] [--hair] [--check] [--collar]`. 15–20 s per iteration; `--check` runs the integrity check below into `DIR/check/`, `--collar` the collar guard into `DIR/collar/`. `SUIT_DEBUG_TAGS=1` colours the jacket's panels (lapel red, collar green, block blue) and `SUIT_NECK_DEBUG=1` prints the neck frame, band and collar columns.
- `bench/m1/suit_check.py`: **integrity check** (added in the fix round, the regression test for defect 3). (a) Silhouette holes: film-transparent renders of body + suit (hair hidden) from 15 directions (8 azimuths, above, below, torso crops), flood-fill the background from the border and count every enclosed background component inside the silhouette (≥ 3 px; the shoe arch band at the ground and anything above the suit's top are ignored). (b) Boundary edges: every suit mesh's boundary loops; for the jacket an edge in the armhole zone (5 cm of the upper-arm segment) fails unless it rests on the jacket's own surface within 6 mm. `--masks RUN` also judges the harness masks (hair on): body views fail on components ≥ 32 px, the close-up (head framing) is reported only. Writes `check.json`, `sil_*.png`, exit 1 on failure. On the baseline (`suitdev/00-baseline/check/`) it found the two shoulder holes in 6 of 15 views (248 px front, 3,488 px in the torso crop) and 5 open armhole edges; on `suit3-final` it passes (the harness masks show only 3–16 px hair slivers at the head).
- `bench/m1/suit_collar.py`: **collar-zone guard** (collar round). On a GLB with hair, beard, brows and lashes hidden: (a) ray-cast ID images from the exact harness close-up camera and three collar crops (front, ¾ at 40°, profile); in the collar zone it counts **bare skin** (body below the shirt band's top with no shirt radially in front of it: skin between the collar pieces), the neck seen inside the band (reported only) and **back faces**, with where-histograms (azimuth round the neck, height over the suprasternale); (b) triangle intersections in the neck zone, per garment and per layer pair (per jacket panel on a `SUIT_DEBUG_TAGS` build), and a radial order test (no shirt outside the jacket). Gates: bare skin 0, back faces ≤ 350 per view, gated intersections ≤ 120 (tie self-intersections and body/shirt reported only), order 0. `--beauty`/`--wire` also render the harness close-up's eye point zoomed onto the collar (`closeup_zoom`, `closeup_wide`, `closeup_far`); `--show jacket,shirt` filters those renders. Writes `collar.json`, `id_*.png`, exit 1 on failure. Baseline (`suitdev/c00-base`) fails with 511 bare-skin px in the close-up and 532 intersections; `collar-final` passes.
- `bench/m1/suit_views.py`: detail views (front, back, back hem, torso, torso ¾, side, lapel, gorge, **notch**, **closeup_h** (the harness close-up's azimuth, collar framing), **collar_back**, **collar_side**, **armpit**, **armpit_back**, knot, shoulder, shoulder back, V bottom, pocket, cuff, hem, hem back, shoe, shoe side, shoe top) at −3 EV; `--wire` overlays the mesh, `--only`/`--hide` filter meshes.
- `bench/m1/suit_pose_test.py`: the poke-through test above.
- `bench/m1/lookdev.sh --config bench/m1/variants/direction.json --prev RUN <label>`: the harness; suit parts show as `tris.parts.suit_*`.
